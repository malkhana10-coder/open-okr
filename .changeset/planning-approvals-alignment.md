Planning has its last two screens. Alignment puts a sector's gaps beside the objectives proposed for them, marks a gap nothing answers, and brings out what a chosen item is tied to. Approvals shows what a sector's next request would hold and what holds it back, sends it, and gives whoever decides the requests waiting for them, to approve or to return with what has to change. Whoever sent a request is not offered its decision.
