A quotation that dropped the "و" or "ف" joined to the first word of a sentence is now verified, and what is kept is the page's wording with the conjunction restored. Only those two letters, only on the first word, and only when the quotation is not on the page as written. Every other word is still matched exactly, and a dropped preposition is still not accepted.
