An objective can now say what it is in the plan: a sector objective or an operational one, a priority or not, new this year or carried over, and whether it comes from a sub-strategy, a proposal or a leadership directive, with a baseline that may still be undetermined. `strategy.listSectorPlan` returns a sector's plan in one list: the objectives of the sector and its agencies with how each is classed, and the operational indicators they follow.
