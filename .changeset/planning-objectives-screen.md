Planning has a third screen, a sector's proposed objectives. Draft objectives asks the planning agent for objectives that answer the sector's gaps, in the cycle chosen. Each arrives as an AI draft with its proposed target, the gaps it answers and the pages it quotes, and with an owner nobody has confirmed. A member confirms the owner. No proposed objective is live until leadership approves it.
