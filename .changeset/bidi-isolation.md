In the Arabic interface, every piece of text now reads in the direction of its own language. A line of English inside the right-to-left page, such as a message not translated yet or a name somebody typed, keeps its number and its full stop at the right ends, and a Latin name inside an Arabic sentence keeps its brackets. English and Malay pages are unchanged.
