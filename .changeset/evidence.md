A gap can now carry its evidence: a quotation, the source document it is from and the page it is on. When the quotation is saved it is checked against the stored text of that page, word for word after Arabic normalisation, and the result is kept: verified, or unverified with the reason. An unverified quotation is kept and shown as such. A quotation from a page that was read poorly says so.
