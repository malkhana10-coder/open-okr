Planning has a second screen, a sector's strategic gaps. A member writes a gap by hand, adds a quotation from a page of the sector's documents, and sees whether the words are on that page. Draft gaps asks the planning agent to draft gaps from the documents: each arrives marked as an AI draft with its quotations checked, and the run reports what it read and what it left out. A gap that is not one is set aside.
